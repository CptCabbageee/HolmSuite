6. What the apps are — and what they are not

Waystead (location reminders)

Waystead's reminders depend on your phone's operating system and on your location settings, and those are not perfect. Battery saving, a poor GPS fix, a permission change, an operating-system update or a powered-off phone can all delay or prevent a reminder.

Do not rely on Waystead for anything where a missed reminder is dangerous or costly — in particular anything to do with medication, medical appointments, legal deadlines or safety. It is a convenience, not a safety system.

Healstead (medication tracker) — read this one

Healstead is a convenience reminder. It is not a medical device, it does not give medical advice, and it must not be relied on as your only way of remembering medication.

It does not know anything about your medicines. It does not check doses, interactions, contraindications or timings. It reminds you of what you typed in — if you enter the wrong dose, it will faithfully remind you of the wrong dose.

A reminder can fail to arrive — a flat battery, a phone that is off, a notification silenced by the operating system, a change to your battery settings. Never treat “no reminder appeared” as “nothing was due”.

Marking a dose as taken is a note you made, not a record of what happened. It is not a clinical record and should not be shown to anyone as one.

Keep whatever system you use today. Healstead is meant to sit alongside a pill organiser, a chart or a pharmacist's schedule — not to replace one. Nothing in Healstead is a substitute for advice from your doctor or pharmacist. For anything about what to take, when, or whether to stop — ask them, not the app.

Third-party information and AI

Route and travel information — including maps, directions, live train times and EV charging points — comes from third parties and can be wrong or out of date. Check the road, not the app.

AI features generate text and can be wrong. Do not treat their output as professional advice. When you use an AI feature, the text of your request is sent to our AI provider to answer it — see the Privacy Policy.

Generated avatars are produced by a third-party image service from the description you type. Ask for something you are happy to send to a third party, and do not include personal information in the description.
