How it went online

  1. 20:21 George, from his phone: "I want to demonstrate to Nina how easily we can get a web page online. Be creative, use an AI image gen."
  2. 20:22 Claude asked OpenAI's image model to paint the village, with this description:
    A cosy storybook illustration of a small village called Holmstead at dusk. Little cottages on a gentle green hill, each with a glowing window in a different colour, linked by a winding path of tiny lanterns. Fireflies, soft mist, a deep indigo starry sky with a crescent moon.
    About 40 seconds later, the painting came back.
  3. 20:23 Claude looked at it, shrank it to 156 KB so it loads fast on a phone, and wrote this page.
  4. Then One pull request to the holmsuite.com repo, an automatic site check, a merge, and GitHub Pages put it live. No servers to set up, no hosting bill, no design tools.

Why the cottages?

Each little house is one of our apps, with its light on: Waystead, Wordstead, Broodstead, Healstead, Witstead and Gradestead. The path of lanterns is what joins them: one account, one wallet of credits, and the Holmstead Hub at the heart of it all.

That's the whole idea of the suite in one picture: separate homes, one village.
