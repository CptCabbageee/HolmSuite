Broodstead

Your family, rewarded: chores with XP, levels and perks.

In development

Broodstead isn’t available to download yet. This page describes the app as it is being built and tested. Want to know when it comes out? Sign up for one email when it launches.

What Broodstead is

Broodstead turns family jobs into a game. Parents set tasks with a reward in XP and coins. Children mark a task done, a parent approves it, and the reward lands. XP takes children up through levels, and coins go on perks the family has agreed.

It is for families who want chores and pocket-money-style rewards without a chart on the fridge.

What it does

  • One family, many people. Create a family, or join one with a six-character invite code. Add children with a PIN, add other adults, and choose who is a parent.
  • Tasks with rewards. Each task has an XP reward, a coin reward, who it is for, and optionally a due date or a repeat.
  • Parent approval. Finished tasks wait as “awaiting approval” until a parent taps Approve or Reject.
  • Levels and perks. Children level up with XP. Perks can be cosmetic, real-world treats, or boosts that multiply rewards.
  • Progress. A family leaderboard and recent activity.
  • AI helpers, paid with credits: turn a chore into a quest, suggest chores, balance jobs fairly, plan a weekly rota, find reward ideas or write a weekly recap.

How it will work, step by step

  1. A parent creates an account. You’ll be asked whether you are 13 or over.
  2. Set up your profile with a display name and an avatar.
  3. Choose Create a family and give it a name.
  4. Tap Add a Child, enter their name and avatar, and choose a four-digit PIN for them.
  5. On Tasks, add a task, choose who it is for and set the XP and coin rewards.
  6. The child switches to their own profile with their PIN and taps Done! when the job is finished.
  7. The parent taps Approve, and the XP and coins are added.

Children and privacy

  • Children under 13 can’t sign up on their own; a parent adds them. A child added by a parent has no login account of their own, just a PIN, and the PIN is stored scrambled.
  • Family information such as names, roles, children’s dates of birth, tasks and rewards is stored on our servers, in London, so everyone in the family sees the same thing.
  • Each family can only see its own information. That is enforced by our servers, not just by the app.
  • The AI helpers send the details they need to Anthropic. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train its models.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

When can I get it?

Broodstead isn’t available yet. Sign up for one email when it launches.

Does my child need an email address?

No. A parent can add a child to the family with a four-digit PIN, and the child switches to their own profile on a shared device.

Who approves tasks?

A parent. A child taps Done! and the reward is only given once a parent approves it.

Can anyone with the invite code make themselves a parent?

No. An adult who joins has to be approved as a parent by an existing parent.

Are there ads for children?

Reward videos are optional. On a child’s profile, only child-appropriate, non-personalised ads can appear.

What are the rewards?

Perks that you set up as a family and hand out yourselves, for example a later bedtime or choosing the film. Broodstead doesn’t pay out money or gift cards.
