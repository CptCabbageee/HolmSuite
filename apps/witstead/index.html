Witstead

Nine fresh puzzles every day, each tuned to the way you think.

Live in your browser Play Witstead in your browser

What Witstead is

Witstead is a daily puzzle game. Every day brings nine fresh puzzles, one of each type, from word hunts and number puzzles to a small crossword. Each type keeps its own difficulty level for you, so getting better at crosswords doesn’t make your number puzzles harder. Puzzles are written with the help of AI, and a built-in puzzle maker steps in whenever the AI isn’t available, so there is always a full set to play.

It suits anyone who likes a short puzzle habit: a few minutes with a coffee, or a longer sit-down going through the whole day’s set.

The nine puzzle types

  • Secret Word: guess the word from the clue.
  • Tile Swap: swap tiles around until the words are right.
  • Four Groups: sort 16 words into four groups that belong together.
  • Sum Guess: work out the hidden equation.
  • Word Trail: trace the themed words hidden in a grid of letters.
  • Wordwit: guess the hidden word, with each guess telling you which letters are right.
  • Crosswit: crack two or three crossing words; a letter you get right carries over into the word that crosses it.
  • Backtrack: you see the answer, and work back to find words that fit each row’s colour pattern.
  • Crossword: fill in a small crossword.

Every puzzle has its own on-screen keyboard, so the board never jumps about when you type. You can hide the keyboard with its button and bring it back when you need it.

Getting started, step by step

  1. Go to holmsuite.com/witstead. Swipe through the short intro, or tap Skip.
  2. Enter the year you were born. Witstead is for people aged 13 and over. Only the yes-or-no answer is kept, not your birth year.
  3. Choose Create account, Sign in, or sign in with Google or Facebook.
  4. Pick a name and an avatar, then tap Start playing.
  5. On the Today screen, choose your three free puzzles for the day, or tap Surprise me.
  6. Tap a puzzle and choose Easy or Hard.
  7. Stuck? Open the clue sheet for a personalised clue, or ask a yes-or-no question.
  8. Finished? Check the Ranks tab to see where you stand this week, and Calendar for days you missed.

Free puzzles, credits and clues

Three puzzles a day are free. In Settings, under Free puzzles, you choose how they are picked: Ask me each day, Same every day or Random. Each puzzle shows how many free ones you have left.

Solving a puzzle earns Holmstead Credits. The faster you solve compared with other players, the more you earn, and Hard mode doubles the reward. A personalised clue costs 10 credits, or you can watch a short video for one instead.

Holmstead Credits (HC) are the suite’s shared wallet. Credits you earn in one Holmstead app can be spent in any of them. More on how they work on the apps page.

The calendar and the weekly leaderboard

The Calendar tab lets you go back and play any past day. A day’s puzzles are the same every time you open them, and the same for everyone, so you can compare notes with a friend.

The Ranks tab shows this week’s top players and your own place. Your score for each puzzle takes into account how hard it was, how quickly you solved it and whether you used a clue. The tab explains the week’s prize rules, and the board starts again each Monday.

Music and settings

Witstead has quiet background music made in-house, in three moods: Calm, Focus and Playful. It starts switched off; turn it on from the music button or in Settings. You can also set a weekly goal and streak reminders.

Your privacy

  • Your account, your name and avatar, which puzzles you have unlocked and your solves are stored on Holmstead’s servers, so your progress follows you and rewards can be checked fairly.
  • Your name and avatar appear on the weekly leaderboard.
  • When you ask for a clue, the puzzle and your wrong guesses are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the clue. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • The optional reward videos on the web version can come from Google. Settings has Ad privacy choices.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

Is Witstead free?

Three puzzles a day are free, and you choose which three. Each extra puzzle unlocks with a short video or 15 Holmstead Credits.

Can I play a day I missed?

Yes. Open the Calendar tab and pick any past day. You get the same puzzles everyone else had that day. Future days stay locked until they arrive.

What is the difference between Easy and Hard?

Easy is the gentlest level and gives you a head start, such as an extra guess or a letter already shown. Hard is tougher and pays double the credits.

Does a clue give the answer away?

No. A clue nudges you towards the answer based on what you have tried so far. Revealing the answer is a separate choice.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Witstead needs a Holmstead account, made with an email address or with Google or Facebook, and you must be 13 or over.

Is there a phone app?

Not in the app stores yet. You can play the full game in your browser today.
