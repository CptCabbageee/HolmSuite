Blockstead

Only your people get through. Everyone else waits.

In development Android only

Blockstead isn’t available to download yet. This page describes the app as it is being built. Want to know when it comes out? Sign up for one email when it launches.

What Blockstead is

Most phones let you silence an app, but not a person. Blockstead works the other way round: you pick your people once, and their messages and calls in your chat apps come through as normal. Notifications from everyone else are quietly taken away and kept in a Held list you can look at whenever you choose.

It is for anyone who wants fewer interruptions without missing the people who matter. It is being built for Snapchat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Messages, Telegram and Discord.

What it does

  • My people. Your one allow-list for every chat app.
  • Watch only, then Block others. Start by just watching, so you can check Blockstead recognises people correctly. Switch to blocking when you are happy.
  • Held. Blocked calls and held messages wait here, each with a button to let that person through.
  • Activity. Who has tried to reach you, with one tap to let them through or always block them.
  • Times. A night setting where only people you have marked can wake you, and “open for business” hours when everyone gets through.
  • Pause for a while when you want everything to come through.
  • Same person, different names. Blockstead can suggest when two names are the same person, using your contacts if you allow it. You decide; it never links them by itself.

How it will work, step by step

  1. Install Blockstead on an Android phone and sign in with email or Google.
  2. Choose what people should call you.
  3. On My people, tap the button to turn on notification access, and switch Blockstead on in your phone’s settings.
  4. Leave it on Watch only for a day or two and check Activity to see that names look right.
  5. Let your people through from Activity, or add them yourself.
  6. Switch to Block others.
  7. If you like, set night and daytime hours on the Times tab.
  8. Check Held whenever you want to catch up.

Your privacy

  • Who messaged you, what they said, your lists and your times stay on your phone. Blockstead never sends them anywhere.
  • Activity keeps only a name, the app and the time. Held keeps message text so you can catch up, on your phone only.
  • Contacts are read only if you allow it, and matching happens on your phone.
  • Our servers hold only your Holmstead account. Settings has a button to delete everything Blockstead has kept on your phone.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

When can I get it?

Blockstead isn’t available yet and is still to be tested on phones. Sign up for one email when it launches.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Apple doesn’t let apps read or hold back other apps’ notifications. On an iPhone, Focus mode is the nearest thing.

Will I miss messages?

No. Messages from people who aren’t on your list aren’t deleted: they wait in the Held list until you look.

Does Blockstead read my messages?

It reads who each notification is from, so it can decide whether to let it through. It keeps the text of held messages so you can catch up, and all of that stays on your phone.

What does Watch only do?

It notes who your notifications are from without blocking anything, so you can check it recognises people correctly before you turn blocking on.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a Holmstead account. The account is the only thing about you that Blockstead keeps on our servers.
