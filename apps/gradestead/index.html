Gradestead

Study partners, with every message checked before anyone sees it.

In development

Gradestead isn’t open yet. This page describes the app as it is being built. Want to know when it comes out? Sign up for one email when it launches.

What Gradestead is

Gradestead helps students find study partners. You create a group for a subject, or find one and join it, and chat with the other members. Revising is easier with someone to test you, and a group is a good place to ask the question you didn’t want to ask in class.

What makes Gradestead different is that safety comes first: every message is checked before anyone else can see it.

What it does

  • My Groups. Create a group with a name and a subject.
  • Discover. Search for groups by name and join them.
  • Group chat. New messages appear as they are sent while the chat is open.
  • Checked before it’s seen. A message that breaks the rules is never posted, and the sender sees why.
  • Report a message for bullying or harassment, sexual content, hate or threats, or something else.
  • Your safety status in Settings shows any warnings, and lets you appeal a ban.

How the safety checks work

The app can’t post a message directly. Every message goes to our server first, which checks that you are a member of the group and aren’t sending too fast, then has the text checked by an automated moderation service. Clean messages are posted. Anything flagged is held back, recorded privately and counts as a warning. Group names and subjects are checked the same way. If the check can’t run for any reason, the message isn’t sent, rather than going out unchecked.

Reports and appeals are looked at by a person. Reports are deleted 12 months after they are closed.

How it will work, step by step

  1. Sign in with your Holmstead account.
  2. Choose the name other members will see.
  3. On My Groups, enter a group name and a subject and tap Create group, or go to Discover, search and tap Join.
  4. Open the group and write your message.
  5. If a message is held back, read the reason. You can see your warnings in Settings.

Questions

When can I use it?

Gradestead isn’t open yet. We are finishing its safety work first. Sign up for one email when it launches.

Who reads my messages?

Every message is checked automatically before anyone else sees it. Once it is posted, the members of that group can read it.

What happens if a message is blocked?

It is never posted. You see why, and it counts as a warning. Three warnings lead to a ban, and the most serious kinds of message lead to a ban straight away.

Can I appeal a ban?

Yes, from Settings. You see the messages that were blocked and can explain why the decision should change.

How do I report someone?

Use the report button on their message and pick a reason. The person you report isn’t told who reported them.
