Bubblestead

Your notes, plans and calendar as see-through bubbles inside bubbles.

Live in your browser Open Bubblestead in your browser

What Bubblestead is

Bubblestead keeps your notes, plans and calendar as see-through 3D bubbles. A bubble can hold notes and other bubbles, as many levels deep as you like, so a “Home” bubble might hold “Garden” and “Kitchen”, each with its own notes. Tap a bubble and you fly into it.

It is for people who think in pictures and places rather than long lists, and who want their notes and dates in one spot. Bubblestead was called Wordstead until October 2026.

What you can do

  • Bubbles inside bubbles. Give each bubble a name, a colour and a picture. Pictures can come from a free search of Wikimedia Commons, from your own photos, or you can have none.
  • Link a bubble so the same bubble also appears inside another one, without copying it.
  • Notes with a title, text, priority, category, due date and time, and attachments. You can lock a note, snooze it or mark it done. Done and deleted notes wait in their own list in case you need them back.
  • A calendar bubble holding years, months and days. Add a note or an event to any day, with repeats, reminders and invitations.
  • AI help with notes: summarise a note, turn it into an outline, rewrite it, tag it, turn a photo of writing into text, or ask a question across all your notes.
  • AI family: describe a project, add files if you like, and the AI plans a whole set of bubbles and notes for you to check before anything is made.
  • Voice dictation: tap the microphone to speak a note instead of typing it.

Getting started, step by step

  1. Go to holmsuite.com/bubblestead. The first time, the page reloads once by itself while it sets up storage in your browser.
  2. Choose Create Account or Sign In, with an email address or with Google or Facebook.
  3. On the home screen, tap New bubble. Name it, choose a colour and a picture, then tap Make it.
  4. Tap your new bubble to fly into it, then tap New note to write your first note.
  5. To plan by date, open the Calendar bubble, then a year, a month and a day, and choose Note on this day or Event on this day. Today takes you straight back to today.
  6. On a phone, tap Add to home screen on the install card to keep Bubblestead one tap away.

Credits

Making a bubble costs 10 Holmstead Credits. The AI note tools also use credits. Planning with AI family is free up to three times a week; you pay only if you choose to make what it plans.

Holmstead Credits (HC) are the suite’s shared wallet. Credits you earn in one Holmstead app can be spent in any of them. More on how they work on the apps page.

Your privacy

  • Your notes and bubbles are stored on your device and, once you agree to upload them, with your account so they sync between devices.
  • Photos you add to your own bubbles, and files you give to AI family, stay on your device.
  • Calendar events are stored with your account so Bubblestead, Holmstead Hub and your other devices show the same calendar. People you invite get one email and never see your email address.
  • AI requests go through our servers to Anthropic. When you turn a photo into text, only the text is kept, not the photo. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Voice dictation happens on your device where it can; otherwise your phone’s own speech service does it. We never receive the audio.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

Is there a phone app?

Not in the app stores yet. The web version works on a phone too: open it in your browser and use Add to home screen to keep it one tap away.

Do bubbles cost credits?

Making a new bubble costs 10 Holmstead Credits. Notes inside bubbles don’t cost anything.

Is planning with AI free?

Planning a set of bubbles with AI is free up to three times a week. You only spend credits if you decide to make the bubbles it suggests.

Will my notes be on my other devices?

Yes. Once you have agreed to upload them, your notes and bubbles are kept with your account, so they appear wherever you sign in. Photos you add to your own bubbles stay on the device you added them on.

Can I use it without the 3D view?

Yes. There is a flat view that works well with screen readers and on older phones.

Is the calendar the same one as in Holmstead Hub?

Yes. Events you add in Bubblestead show in Holmstead Hub, and the other way round.
