Holmstead Hub

One sign-in, one wallet of credits and your whole day in one place.

Live in your browser Open Holmstead Hub in your browser

What the Hub is

Holmstead Hub is home base for the Holmstead apps. It shows your credits, your level and skill tree, a calendar you share with Bubblestead, and what is waiting for you in your other apps. It is also where you meet Ask Holm, the helper you can type to from any Holmstead app.

If you use more than one Holmstead app, the Hub is the quickest way to see your whole day in one place.

What you can do

  • What’s my day? Tap Brief me for a short AI summary of your day across the apps. A brief costs 20 credits.
  • See what’s waiting: cards show things like how many reminders you have active or whether today’s puzzle is still to play.
  • Ask Holm: type a command or a question. See below.
  • Calendar: events, reminders and invitations, shared with Bubblestead. On a phone you can also link your phone’s own calendars, which the Hub reads but never changes.
  • Credits, level and skill tree: your balance, ways to earn free credits, and the rewards you unlock as you level up.
  • Your apps: a list that opens each Holmstead app or its page.

Ask Holm

Ask Holm is a floating button in every Holmstead app once you are signed in. Some commands are instant, free and never use AI:

  • what’s on or today: your day at a glance.
  • credits: your balance.
  • note: …: save a quick note.
  • search …: look through your things.
  • open followed by an app’s name: jump to that app.

Anything else goes to the AI and costs credits. Holm only sees a short summary to answer with: reminder titles, chores, up to six note titles and, for medicines, only a count.

Getting started, step by step

  1. Go to holmsuite.com/holmstead. The first time, the page reloads once by itself.
  2. Choose Create Account or Sign In, with an email address or with Google or Facebook. The same account works in every Holmstead app.
  3. Start at What’s my day? and tap Brief me when you want a summary.
  4. Try Ask Holm: type what’s on or credits.
  5. Tap Calendar, choose a day and tap New event.
  6. Tap your credits to see your balance and the free ways to earn more.
  7. Scroll to Your apps to open another Holmstead app.

Holmstead Credits (HC) are the suite’s shared wallet. Credits you earn in one Holmstead app can be spent in any of them. More on how they work on the apps page.

Your privacy

  • Calendar events are stored with your account. Linked phone calendars are read, never changed, and unlinking one removes its events from our servers.
  • Ask Holm questions and daily briefs go through our servers to Anthropic. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Email sign-in includes an invisible check that you are not a robot, run by Cloudflare.
  • Reward videos only play when you choose to watch one.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

Is Ask Holm free?

Its quick commands, such as “what’s on”, “credits” or “note: …”, are free and never use AI. Questions that need an AI answer cost credits.

Can the Hub read my notes?

No. For your daily brief and Ask Holm it sees only the titles of up to six Bubblestead notes that are due or urgent, not what is inside them.

Is the calendar the same as Bubblestead’s?

Yes. It is one calendar, shared between the two apps and your devices.

Can I invite people to an event?

Yes, by email. They get one email with the details and links to answer yes, maybe or no, and they never see your email address.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings. Your account is deleted after 14 days, and signing in during those 14 days cancels it. You can also use our delete your account page.
