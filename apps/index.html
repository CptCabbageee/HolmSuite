The Holmstead apps

Everyday apps that share one account and one wallet of credits.

Every Holmstead app does one everyday job. They share one account, one wallet of credits and one level, so the more of them you use, the more they help each other. Each one still works on its own.

Use them in your browser today

Witstead

Live in your browser

A daily puzzle game: nine fresh puzzles every day, from word hunts and number puzzles to a small crossword, each tuned to you.

Bubblestead

Live in your browser

Notes, plans and a calendar as see-through 3D bubbles inside bubbles. Tap a bubble to fly in.

Holmstead Hub

Live in your browser

Home base for the suite: your daily brief, Ask Holm, a shared calendar, your credits and your level.

How the apps fit together

One account

One Holmstead account signs you in to every app. You can make it with an email address, or with Google or Facebook.

Holmstead Credits

Holmstead Credits, or HC, are the suite’s shared wallet. You earn them by doing things in the apps, such as solving Witstead puzzles, by levelling up, by watching an optional reward video, or as a thank-you when a problem you reported gets fixed. You spend them on extras that cost us something to run, mostly AI features, and on things like making a new bubble in Bubblestead.

Credits you earn are shared across every Holmstead app. Credits you buy can only be spent in the app you bought them in. Buying credits isn’t available yet, so for now every credit is an earned one.

Ask Holm

Ask Holm is a helper in every app once you are signed in. Quick commands like “what’s on” or “credits” are free; questions that need AI cost credits. More on the Holmstead Hub page.

One calendar

Bubblestead and Holmstead Hub share one calendar, so an event you add in one shows in the other and on all your devices.

For venues

We also make Tapstead, a loyalty card and events app for independent bars and cafés.
