Waystead

Reminders that go off when you arrive somewhere, or leave.

In development

Waystead isn’t available to download yet. This page describes the app as it is being built and tested. Want to know when it comes out? Sign up for one email when it launches.

What Waystead is

Waystead gives you reminders tied to places instead of times. Pin a spot on the map, write what you need to remember, and your phone tells you when you get there, or when you leave. “Pick up the parcel” goes off at the post office, not at 3pm while you are still at work. Each reminder is called a waystead.

It is for adults who keep thinking “I must remember that when I’m there”. Because it uses your location in the background, Waystead is for people aged 18 and over.

What it does

  • Arrive or leave. Each waystead goes off when you arrive at a place or when you leave it. Choose how big the area is, from 150 metres to a kilometre.
  • Search or tap the map. Find a shop or a street by searching, or zoom in and tap the exact spot.
  • Only when it suits you. Limit a waystead to certain hours or days, or have it go off at a set time only if you are at the place then.
  • Leaving home. Set your home and get a reminder as you leave: keys, lunch, the library books.
  • Errands. Keep a list of things to pick up or take with you, and a standing list for the things you always need.
  • Say it in your own words. Describe a reminder in plain words and let AI fill in the details, or use the microphone to dictate it.
  • Battery help. Settings has a Fix Battery Optimisation button and a self-check that tests your reminders will really go off.

How it will work, step by step

  1. Sign in with your Holmstead account, by email or with Google or Facebook.
  2. Allow location, including in the background. Without background location, reminders can’t go off while the app is closed.
  3. Set your home address, by searching or by tapping the map.
  4. On the map, search for a place or zoom in until you can see the right street.
  5. Tap the spot, then Add.
  6. Type what you want to remember, choose Arrive or Leave, set the size of the area and save it.
  7. In Settings, tap Fix Battery Optimisation so your phone doesn’t hold your reminders back.

Your privacy

  • Your phone’s own location system watches for your places. Waystead doesn’t stream your position to us, and no trail of where you have been is stored.
  • Your places, reminders, errands and home address are stored with your account so they are safe if you change phone.
  • Map, search and route requests go to Google Maps Platform. The weather warning uses a rough area several kilometres wide, never your exact location.
  • Waystead counts anonymous events, such as “the app was opened”, with Aptabase. They carry no name, email or account identifier.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

When can I get it?

Waystead isn’t in the Play Store yet. It is being tested before release. Sign up for one email when it launches.

Will it drain my battery?

Waystead asks your phone’s own location system to watch for your places, rather than tracking you itself. To save battery, phones can be a minute or two late noticing that you have left somewhere.

How small can a place be?

The smallest area you can set reaches 150 metres from the spot you pin. Smaller areas don’t trigger reliably on phones.

Does Waystead keep a record of where I go?

No. Your phone tells the app when you arrive at or leave one of your own places. No trail of your movements is stored.

Who can see my journeys?

Only people you invite to follow a journey, and they see the names of your stops and the times, never a map position.

Which phones will it work on?

Android first. There is no iPhone or web version yet.
