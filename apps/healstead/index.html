Healstead

Remember your medicines, and keep a clear record of every dose.

In development

Healstead isn’t available to download yet. This page describes the app as it is being built and tested. Want to know when it comes out? Sign up for one email when it launches.

What Healstead is

Healstead is a medication tracker. Add the medicines you take, set when you take them, and Healstead reminds you at the right time. Tap Taken or Skipped for each dose and it builds up a clear record you can look back on or show your doctor.

It is for people looking after their own regular medicines who want fewer “did I take it?” moments. Healstead is not a medical device and doesn’t give medical advice; it tells you so before you start.

What it does

  • Find your medicine by searching a built-in list by name, or by scanning the barcode on the pack. The list is stored on your phone.
  • Reminders at the times you choose, on the days you choose.
  • Taken or skipped for each dose, with a check if you try to mark a dose you have already taken today.
  • A nudge if a dose hasn’t been marked after a while you choose.
  • Your week at a glance, and your full dose history.
  • Running low? Track how many doses you have left and get a warning before you run out.
  • Travel: keep reminders on home time while you are away.
  • Notes for your GP, emergency information such as allergies, and exports of your history and your medicines.
  • App lock with your phone’s fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Optional AI help, only after you agree: a plain-words explanation of a medicine, or help preparing for an appointment.

How it will work, step by step

  1. Sign in with your Holmstead account, by email or with Google or Facebook.
  2. Read the “Before you start” notice and tap I understand.
  3. Allow notifications, so reminders can reach you.
  4. Go to Meds and tap Search by Name, or Scan a barcode. Choose your medicine and add its strength if you like.
  5. Add a reminder time and choose which days it repeats.
  6. If you want a low-stock warning, turn on refill tracking and enter how many doses you have.
  7. Each day, tap Taken or Skipped on the Today screen.
  8. Look at Log to see your week and your dose history.

Your privacy

  • Your medicines, doses, schedules, supply counts and notes are stored on your phone, not on our servers. Reminders are set on your phone too.
  • Android’s cloud backup leaves your medicine records out.
  • Information leaves your phone only when you choose a feature that needs it: for example, if you turn on an alert for family members when you miss a dose, or use an AI feature. AI features send what they need to Anthropic only after you agree. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Reward videos are optional and never personalised in Healstead.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Questions

When can I get it?

Healstead isn’t available yet. Sign up for one email when it launches.

Is Healstead medical advice?

No. Healstead helps you remember and record your medicines. It doesn’t give dosing advice or check how medicines interact. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist.

Can a reminder be missed?

Yes, it can. Battery saving, missing permissions, or a flat or switched-off phone can stop a reminder. Healstead can nudge you if a dose hasn’t been marked, but don’t rely on any app alone for a medicine you must not miss.

Does it work without internet?

Logging doses and reminders work on your phone without a connection. Signing in and the AI features need one.

Can I share my history with my doctor?

Yes. You can export your dose history and your current medicines, and prepare a summary before an appointment.
