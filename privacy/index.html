Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Holmstead apps: Waystead, Witstead, Wordstead, Healstead, Broodstead and the Holmstead hub. They share one account, so they share one policy.

The short version

  • We collect the account you sign in with, the places and reminders you create, and — only if you allow it — your location.
  • Our phone apps use no advertising or tracking SDKs. On Witstead’s web version only, the optional “watch a video for credits” ads can come from Google (see below). Waystead counts anonymous usage events (for example “the app was opened” or “a feature was used”) with Aptabase; these carry no name, email or account identifier.
  • We do not sell your data, and we never pass your account, email or what you do in our apps to advertisers.
  • Your location is used to tell your own device when you arrive somewhere.
  • You can delete your account, from inside the app or by email. Four small records are kept afterwards, and they are listed below rather than buried.

Who we are

The Holmstead apps are made and run by George Michael Batty, an individual developer trading as Holmstead. That means I am the data controller — the person legally responsible for how your data is handled.

You can contact me at privacy@holmsuite.com.

This policy says “we” in most places simply because it reads more naturally, but Holmstead is one person.

What we collect

Account. An email address, and — if you sign in with Google or Facebook — the account identifier that provider gives us, plus your name and profile picture where you have chosen to share them. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, our hosting and database provider. We never see or store your password for Google or Facebook.

What you create. The places you save, your reminders and errands, your saved home address, journeys and routes you plan, your settings, and your avatar.

Journey history. When you travel a saved Waystead journey, we record the time you leave and arrive at each of its named stops: the stop’s name and the time, never a map position. This builds your journey recap (“You left the office at 5:12pm and got home at 5:41pm”).

Photos you choose to send. Some features use a photo you pick or take: reading a medicine label in Healstead, turning a photo attached to a note into text in Wordstead, and using your own photo as a wallpaper in Waystead. The photo goes to our servers and to Anthropic, who read the text or check the picture is suitable. Healstead and Wordstead keep only the text that comes back, not the photo. Waystead keeps the wallpaper you chose so it can show it to you, and deletes it with your account. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days and does not use it to train its models.

Location. Precise location, and — only with your separate permission — location while the app is closed or not in use. This is what makes an arrival reminder possible at all: the app cannot tell you “you are near the shop” without knowing you are near the shop.

Your suite balance. Holmstead Credits, level and progress. One account is shared across every Holmstead app, and so are the credits you earn: a credit earned in one app is spendable in another. Credits bought in a credit pack can only be spent in the app they were bought in.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your device — an identifier the notification services use to reach your phone. It is stored against your account so reminders and updates can be delivered to you, and it is deleted with your account.

Problem reports. If you use “Report an issue” in the app, we store what you write, along with the app version, the platform (Android or iOS) and which screen you were on. This is so we can find and fix the problem. Please do not include anything sensitive in the description.

Problem reports. If you tap “Report a problem”, we receive what you write, a screenshot of the screen you were on (unless you switch it off), and the app, screen, app version and device or browser type. Text boxes and personal details such as names and email addresses are blurred in the screenshot on your device before it is sent. We use reports only to find and fix problems. Reports and screenshots are deleted automatically after 30 days, and straight away if you delete your account (screenshots within 30 days). If you ask us to, we’ll email you once when the problem is fixed: at your account’s address, or at an address you give us if you’re not signed in. We use it only for that one email, and delete an address you gave us once it’s sent (or after 30 days). When we fix a problem you reported, we may add a few Holmstead Credits to your account as a thank-you. If you weren’t signed in, we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from the email address you gave us (not the address itself) for up to 90 days, so we can give you the credits when you sign in to a Holmstead app with that address. After that it’s deleted.

Voice dictation. Waystead and Wordstead let you tap a microphone to speak instead of type. Speech is turned into text on your phone where your phone supports it. Where it does not, your phone’s own operating system sends the audio to Apple’s or Google’s speech service to do it. We never receive, store or hear the audio, only the text, which becomes the reminder, setting or note you were writing.

Usage events. Waystead sends anonymous counts of what happens in the app to Aptabase, such as the app being opened or a feature being used, together with the app version and type of device. Aptabase works out your country and region from your connection’s IP address and then discards the address. No name, email or account identifier is included.

Interest in purchases. Which credit pack or plan you tap while purchases aren’t available yet, so we know what people want. It is deleted with your account.

Web versions. If you use one of our apps in a web browser, your sign-in is kept in that browser’s own storage so you stay signed in, until you sign out or clear it.

Calendar and invitations. Events you add to the calendar in Holmstead Hub or Wordstead (title, time, repeats, reminders, place and notes) are stored against your account so both apps and your other devices show the same calendar. Reminders are set on your phone. If you invite someone, we store their email address, your display name, and their answer (yes, maybe or no) with the event, and send them one email with the event details and links to answer. We don’t show them your email address. If they have a Holmstead account with that address, the invitation also appears in their apps. Only you and the people you invite can see an event. When you delete an event, its details and its invitations are wiped from our servers straight away; we keep only a blank marker so your other devices know it was deleted. Everything here is removed when you delete your account, and an invitation is also removed when you take the person off it. The emails are sent by Resend. The lawful basis is providing the calendar you asked for, and for invitations, our legitimate interest in delivering an invitation you chose to send.

Technical data. Standard server logs from our providers.

Launch alert emails (holmsuite.com). If you ask on holmsuite.com to be emailed when an app comes out, we keep your email address, the apps and phones (Android or iPhone) you picked, and when you signed up and confirmed. We use them only to send you the emails you asked for: one email to confirm, then one when each app you picked comes out on your phone. Every email has a link to stop them; using it deletes your details straight away. If you never confirm, we delete your details after 7 days. The sign-up form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check you’re not a robot, which sees your IP address and browser details for that check. Emails are sent by Resend. The lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the link in any email.

Tapstead requests (holmsuite.com/tapstead). If you use the “Get Tapstead for your bar” form, we collect what you type: your venue’s name, your name, email address, phone number if you give one, town and message. We send it straight to our own inbox as one email and use it only to reply to you about Tapstead. We don’t keep it in a database or anywhere else, and we delete the email once it’s no longer needed. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check you’re not a robot, which sees your IP address and browser details for that check. The email is sent by Resend. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering a business enquiry you sent us.

Location — what actually leaves your device

This section is deliberately specific, because “we collect location” is true of both a geofence and a tracking network, and they are not the same thing.

  • Geofencing is done by your device's own operating system. You give the app a list of places you care about; Android or iOS watches for you entering or leaving them and wakes the app up. A continuous stream of your position is not sent to us.
  • The places you save are stored on our servers, because they sync between your devices and across the suite.
  • Map, search and route features involve Google. The map itself is drawn on your device by Google's mapping component, so Google receives your map activity directly from your phone. Place searches and route requests are sent from our servers rather than from your device.
  • Background location is used for arrival reminders and journey departure reminders only. It is not used to build a history of where you have been, beyond the journey history described above.
  • We do not record the path you actually travelled. The line drawn along a saved journey is the planned route, not a trace of where you went. While the map is open the app watches your position to show your position marker, but keeps only the single previous reading, in memory, to work out whether you are moving and which way you are facing. Apart from the journey history above, nothing about your movement is written down or sent to us.
  • Followers, only if you invite them. Waystead lets you share an invite code with someone. Only if they accept it can they see your journey recaps. Either of you can end it at any time. This is the only way another person ever sees anything about you through the apps.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and what you create for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed — see Deleting your account, and the four exceptions listed below.

Children

Waystead is intended for adults (18 or over). Our apps are not all aimed at the same audience: Broodstead is designed for family use and sets its own age terms. Each app states its own minimum age, and each is declared separately on its app store listing.

Why we are allowed to use your data

Data-protection law requires us to name a lawful basis for each thing we do with your data.

  • Your location — including background location — is used on the basis of your consent. You choose whether to grant it, and you can withdraw it at any time, in the app's settings or in your phone's own settings. If you withdraw it, arrival reminders stop working, but nothing else about your account is affected and you are not penalised for it.
  • Running your account — signing you in, storing what you create, syncing it between your devices — is on the basis of performing our contract with you: it is the service you asked for.
  • Preventing abuse of sign-up rewards is on the basis of our legitimate interests in not having the same person claim a reward repeatedly.
  • Making sure one person wins at most one Witstead weekly prize is also on the basis of our legitimate interests. When prizes are paid, accounts with the same email address (after the tidying described under “Deleting your account” below) or the same Google or Facebook login count as one person.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Email privacy@holmsuite.com.

If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to a regulator — in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

Two routes, and both do the same thing:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Without installing anything: email privacy@holmsuite.com from the address your account uses, and ask us to delete it.

You get at least 14 days to change your mind. When you ask us to delete your account we schedule it for 14 days' time and tell you the date — or, if you have a subscription with longer than that left to run, for the end of the month you have paid for, which is never more than 28 days. Nothing is removed during those 14 days — sign in and choose “Keep my account” and everything is exactly as you left it. If you do not cancel, the deletion completes automatically, always within the 30 days we have to act on an erasure request.

Deletion removes your account and the data attached to it — your saved places, reminders, routes, home address, journey history, followers, settings, avatar, notification token, problem reports, balance and progress.

What we keep after you delete, and why

Four records survive deletion. We would rather list them than let you find out later.

  • A one-way hash of the email address that claimed a sign-up reward. A one-way hash is a scrambled fingerprint — the original address cannot be recovered from it, and it cannot be used to contact you. Before hashing we lowercase the address, drop anything after a + sign, and remove dots from Gmail addresses, so variations of the same inbox count as one.
  • The provider account identifier, if a sign-up reward was claimed through Google or Facebook. This is the anonymous account number that provider gives us — not your name, email or profile.
  • If you were granted a founder reward for a suggestion, the record of that grant — including the email address it was awarded to, in full. This one is not hashed, and we would rather say so plainly than describe it vaguely. It is kept so the reward can still be honoured if you come back, and so the same address cannot claim it twice.
  • If we owe you a refund for credits you paid for and did not spend, the record of that refund — the Google Play order number, the amount, and an internal reference to your deleted account. We keep it because we cannot pay you back after your account is gone if the record of what we owe you goes with it. It contains no reminders, locations or personal content, and it only exists if you actually paid us and had credits left over.

The first two exist to stop one person claiming a sign-up reward over and over. None of the four is used for marketing, analytics or profiling.

The lawful basis for the refund record is our legal obligation, not your consent and not our convenience: a refund is a financial transaction, and UK tax law requires us to keep the record of it. That means it is the one record on this list we cannot delete on request while the six years are running.

How long we keep them: the first three indefinitely, the refund record for six years. That is deliberate rather than an oversight. The whole point of the first two is that one person can claim the sign-up reward once, ever — a record that expired would move the loophole rather than close it. The founder-reward record is kept so we can still honour the reward whenever you come back.

You can ask us to delete the founder-reward record anyway. Email privacy@holmsuite.com and say so, and we will remove it. It means we can no longer honour that reward if you return.
