Common questions

Do I need a separate account for each app?

No. One Holmstead account signs you in to every Holmstead app, and your Holmstead Credits are shared between them.

How do I delete my account?

In any Holmstead app go to Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps on our delete your account page, which works without installing anything.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so you cancel them there: open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, pick the subscription and tap Cancel subscription. On a computer, use your Google Play subscriptions page. Uninstalling an app or deleting your Holmstead account does not cancel a Play subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Email us and we will look at it. You can also ask Google Play directly under Google's own refund process. Nothing here affects your statutory rights.
