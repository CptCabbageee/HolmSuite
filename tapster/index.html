a loyalty app for your bar

Your own stamp card, free-drink rewards and gig guide, on your customers' phones. There's no app store: they open your page, sign in with their email, and add it to their home screen.

What it does

  • 🏷️Digital stamp cardEach customer gets their own QR code. Staff scan it at the bar and the stamp appears on the customer's phone straight away.
  • 🍺Free-drink rewardsA full card earns a free one, and the scanner tells staff when it's due. You choose how many stamps a card needs.
  • 🎸Gigs and eventsYour upcoming nights with posters and ticket links, kept up to date by your own staff from their phones.
  • 🔔AlertsCustomers who turn them on get a notification from you when there's a new gig or event to shout about.
  • 🍻On tapShow what's pouring now and what's coming soon. The tab only appears once you've added a drink.
  • 📊Your numbersDrinks stamped today and this week, free drinks given, sign-ups, your busiest days, and scans per member of staff.
  • 🎨Make it yoursYour logo, your tagline and your own names for the tabs, changed from the admin screen.
  • 📱Works on any phoneIt runs in the phone's web browser, iPhone or Android. Nothing to download from a store.

Built with UK licensing rules in mind: only a few drinks a day count towards the card, so rewards are for coming back, not for one big night. Customers can download their data or delete their account themselves.
