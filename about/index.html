About Holmstead

A small, independent UK app studio making everyday apps that work together.

Who we are

Holmstead is a small, independent app studio in the UK. It is one person: George Michael Batty, an individual developer trading as Holmstead. When our pages say “we”, that is mostly because it reads more naturally. If you email us, a real person reads it.

What the suite is for

Most of us juggle a dozen apps that don’t know about each other: one for notes, one for reminders, one for the calendar, one for the kids’ chores. Each wants its own account, and none of them helps the others.

The Holmstead Suite is an attempt at the opposite. Each app does one everyday job well, and they all share one account, one wallet of credits and one level. Your notes can show up in your daily brief. A puzzle solved in Witstead earns credits you can spend in Bubblestead. You can ask Holm what’s on today from any of them. But every app still stands on its own, so you only use the ones you want.

The name comes from homestead: a home with everything it needs around it. Every app in the suite is a “-stead”: Waystead for places, Witstead for puzzles, Bubblestead for notes, Healstead for medicines, Broodstead for families, and more on the apps page.

How we build

  • Privacy you can read. Our phone apps use no tracking SDKs. We don’t sell your data, and we never pass your account, your email or what you do in our apps to advertisers. Our privacy policy lists what we keep in plain words, including what is kept after you delete your account.
  • Health data stays home. Healstead keeps your medicine records on your phone, not on our servers. Blockstead keeps who messaged you, and what they said, on your phone.
  • Easy to tell apart. In September 2026 we chose new colours for every app so they are easier to tell apart for people with colour blindness, and Bubblestead has a flat, screen-reader-friendly view alongside its 3D one.
  • Honest about AI. AI costs real money every time it runs, so AI features cost credits, and some come with a free allowance, such as Witstead’s three free puzzles a day or three free AI plans a week in Bubblestead. When an app sends something to an AI provider, our privacy policy says what and to whom.
  • Earned, not bought. You can earn credits by using the apps, and reward videos only play when you choose to watch one.

Where things stand

Three apps work in your browser today: Witstead, Bubblestead and Holmstead Hub. The others are being built and tested, and none of the apps is in an app store yet. Our news page lists what has changed recently, with dates.

Alongside the suite we make Tapstead, a loyalty card and events app for independent bars and cafés.
