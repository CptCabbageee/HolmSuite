Blockstead

Only the people you choose can reach you — everyone else gets held, not blocked outright, until you decide.

Why this page isn't an app demo: blocking notifications happens inside your phone, which a web browser can't reach. So this page shows how to get most of the way there yourself, right now, app by app, while the Blockstead app for Android is built. Each step was checked against the app's own help page on 22 October 2026. Apps move their menus around, so if a name differs slightly, search the app's settings for it.

Snapchat

  1. Tap your profile picture, then the gear (Settings).
  2. Under privacy controls, tap Contact Me.
  3. Choose Friends. Only people you've added back can send you Snaps, Chats or calls.
  4. To stop calls from non-friends ringing, also check the notification settings, which Snapchat says work alongside Contact Me.

People in a group chat with you can still reach you in that group.

Checked against Snapchat Support: Who can contact me on Snapchat?

WhatsApp

  1. Settings → Privacy → Calls, then turn on Silence unknown callers. Calls from numbers not in your contacts stop ringing.
  2. Settings → Privacy → Groups, then choose My contacts (or My contacts except…). Strangers can't add you to groups any more.
  3. For one person who won't stop: open the chat, tap their name, then Block.

Group settings only stop direct adds. Someone can still send you an invite link, which you can ignore.

Checked against WhatsApp Help: Silence unknown callers and How to change group privacy settings

Instagram

  1. Messages from people you don't follow arrive as requests, not in your main inbox. Accept, decline, or decline them all at once.
  2. Turn on Hidden Words for message requests, so likely scams and offensive requests go straight to a hidden folder. It's off by default.
  3. Story replies: by default anyone who sees your story can reply. Change who can reply in your settings.

Checked against Instagram Help: Manage message requests, Hide message requests you don't want to see and Manage who can reply to your story

Messenger

  1. Tap your profile picture, then Privacy & safety → Message delivery.
  2. For each group of people (friends of friends, people with your number, everyone else), choose Message requests or Don't receive requests.
  3. For one person: Restrict them (no notifications, and your phone won't ring when they call) or Block them.

Someone you've never chatted with can't call you until you reply to their request.

Checked against Messenger Help: Control who can message you and Restrict someone

Messages (iMessage / SMS)

iPhone

  1. Settings → Apps → Messages, scroll to Unknown Senders, turn on Screen Unknown Senders. Texts from people not in your contacts go to their own folder, with no notification.
  2. Settings → Apps → Phone → Screen Unknown Callers: choose Silence to send unknown numbers to voicemail, or Ask Reason for Calling.

Save anyone you want to hear from as a contact first, so they aren't caught.

Android (Google Messages)

  1. Spam protection is on by default: Messages settings → Protection & safety.
  2. To block someone: press and hold their conversation, tap Block. They aren't told.

Checked against Apple: Screen and filter text messages, Apple: Manage unknown callers and Google Messages: Block senders

Telegram

  1. Settings → Privacy and Security → Calls: choose My Contacts (or Nobody), then add exceptions for anyone else you want.
  2. Settings → Privacy and Security → Messages: limit who can message you to My Contacts.
  3. Blocked people can't message you or add you to groups.

A public username lets people find and message you without your number. Remove it if you don't need it.

Checked against Telegram FAQ. Exact menu names here are less certain than for the other apps; check your app version.

Discord

  1. User Settings → Privacy & Safety (called Content & Social in newer versions): turn off Allow direct messages from server members. Choose Yes when asked whether to apply it to servers you're already in.
  2. Friend Requests: untick Everyone, and keep only Friends of Friends (or nothing).
  3. Message Requests is on for everyone: messages from non-friends go to a separate requests inbox.

Checked against Discord Support: Blocking & Privacy Settings and Message Requests

iPhone Focus setup

The closest an iPhone gets to Blockstead: a Focus that only lets your people through.

  1. Settings → Focus, then tap + to make a new Focus (or use Do Not Disturb).
  2. Under People, choose Allow Notifications From, then add the people you always want to hear from.
  3. On the same screen, set Allow calls from to Allowed People Only.
  4. Turn the Focus on from Control Centre, or set it to switch on by itself on a schedule.

Calls from your emergency contacts always come through.

Checked against Apple Support: Allow or silence notifications for a Focus and Set up a Focus
