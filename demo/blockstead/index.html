What Blockstead does

♥

One list of your people

Pick the people who matter once. They get through on Snapchat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, texts, Telegram and Discord.

☎

A blocked calls list

Calls from anyone else don't ring. You can see exactly who tried, and when.

⌂

Held, not lost

Nothing is deleted. Everything waits in Held, and one tap lets someone through for good.

🔒

Stays on your phone

No account and no uploads. Who messaged you, and what they said, never leaves your phone.
