What Blockstead does

One list of your people

Pick the people who matter once. They get through on Snapchat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, texts, Telegram and Discord.

A blocked calls list

Calls from anyone else don't ring. You can see exactly who tried, and when.

Held, not lost

Nothing is deleted. Everything waits in Held, and one tap lets someone through for good.

Who messaged you, and what they said, never leaves your phone.

Who makes Blockstead

Blockstead is made by Holmstead, an independent app maker in Ipswich, England. It’s one of a family of everyday apps that share one account. It’s still in development: nothing is for sale yet, and this page is a preview.

Why it needs notification access

To decide whether a message should buzz, Blockstead needs to see who it’s from. It uses Android’s own notification access, which you switch on yourself in Settings and can switch off at any time. It works on your phone: who messaged you, and what they said, never leaves it.
