A few other things it'll do:

📅

Drag a note anywhere on the board, or into a day on the built-in calendar

⏰

Turn a note into a reminder that fires on the date you pin it to

🎨

Colour-code boards per project, so work notes and home notes don't mix

🔒

Everything stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up
