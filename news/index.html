News

What has changed in the Holmstead apps and on this site, newest first.

25 November 2026

Guides for every app

Every Holmstead app now has its own page on this site, saying what it does, how to get started, how it handles your data, and answers to common questions. Start at the apps page. There is also a new About page, and this news page.

23 November 2026

Privacy policy updated

Our privacy policy now explains when data goes to providers outside the UK and the safeguards used, how to complain (to us first, then to the Information Commissioner’s Office), and that Google AdMob supplies the optional reward videos in our phone apps.

19 November 2026

Witstead shows your free puzzles left

Each Witstead puzzle now shows how many of your three free puzzles for the day you have left.

17 November 2026

A new Holmstead logo

The homepage has a new animated Holmstead logo at its centre, and the site has a new icon to match.

12 November 2026

Witstead names its own puzzles

Witstead’s puzzle types now have names of their own: Secret Word, Tile Swap, Four Groups, Sum Guess, Word Trail, Wordwit, Crosswit, Backtrack and Crossword. The Witstead guide explains each one.

8 November 2026

Report a problem from any page

Every page on this site now has a “Report a problem” button. You don’t need an account, and an email address is optional; leave one if you would like to hear when it is fixed.

7 November 2026

Holmstead Hub opens in your browser

Holmstead Hub, home base for the suite, now works in your browser at holmsuite.com/holmstead: your daily brief, Ask Holm, the shared calendar and your credits.

30 October 2026

Bubblestead opens in your browser

Bubblestead now works in your browser at holmsuite.com/bubblestead, with your notes, plans and calendar as 3D bubbles you fly into.

28 October 2026

Wordstead becomes Bubblestead

Our notes app changed its name from Wordstead to Bubblestead, to match the see-through bubbles that now hold your notes. The old Wordstead address now forwards to Bubblestead.

22 October 2026

Introducing Blockstead

We announced Blockstead, a people-only notifications app for Android: choose your people once, and chat notifications from everyone else wait in a Held list.

14 October 2026

Witstead: pick your free puzzles, and music

In Witstead you can now choose which three puzzles are free each day, or let it choose at random. Easy mode gives you a head start, and there is optional background music in three moods.

12 October 2026

A support page

Our support page went live, with how to reach us, answers to common questions, and links to our policies.

10 October 2026

Play Witstead in your browser

Witstead, our daily puzzle game, became the first Holmstead app you can use on the web, at holmsuite.com/witstead.
