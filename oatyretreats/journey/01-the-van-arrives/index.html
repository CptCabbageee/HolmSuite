Episode 1: The van arrives

She's here! An empty panel van rolls onto the drive, and the Oaty's Retreats adventure officially begins. First impressions, the plan, and a lot of nervous excitement.

Well, that’s it — there’s no going back now. A big empty panel van is sat on my drive, and it’s mine. This is where Oaty’s Retreats really begins.

First impressions

Standing inside an empty van for the first time is a strange feeling. It’s bigger than it looks from outside, and completely, echoingly empty. Every surface is a decision waiting to be made.

Here’s what’s running through my head:

  • Where does the bed go — fixed, or a clever fold-away?
  • How much power do I actually need to live off-grid?
  • Can I really fit a kitchen, a bed, and somewhere to sit?

Measure twice, cut once. Right now I’ve measured nothing and I’m terrified of cutting anything.

The plan for the build

Before I touch a single tool, the next few weeks are all about planning. I’ll be sketching layouts, pricing up materials, and — most importantly — figuring out what I don’t know yet.

Follow along and we’ll learn this together. Next episode: stripping her back, cleaning up, and getting to bare metal ready for insulation.
