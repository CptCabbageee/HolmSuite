Episode 2: Insulation, and my first proper mistake

Fluff everywhere, a heap of lessons, and one job I had to do twice. This week is all about keeping the van warm — and my ego humble.

If Episode 1 was all excitement, Episode 2 is where the reality kicked in. This week was insulation — and it was messier, itchier and more educational than I bargained for.

Why insulation matters so much

A metal box is freezing in winter and an oven in summer. Insulation is what turns it into somewhere you’d actually want to sleep. Get it wrong and you get condensation, damp, and a mouldy van. No thanks.

The mistake

I’ll be honest with you: I did a whole section, stood back proudly, then realised I’d left cold bridges all along the ribs. Had to pull half of it back out and start again.

  • Lesson one: don’t rush the fiddly bits to feel like you’re making progress.
  • Lesson two: a cheap respirator and goggles are worth every penny.
  • Lesson three: the van will humble you. Repeatedly.

Doing it in the open like this means you get to watch me get it wrong first — so hopefully you won’t have to.

Next up: ply-lining and the first bits of framework going in. It’s finally starting to feel like something.
