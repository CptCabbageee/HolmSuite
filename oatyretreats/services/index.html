How it’ll work

From empty shell to home on wheels, at your pace.

01

A cuppa & a plan

We sit down (in person or on a call) and figure out how you want to use your van, your budget, and what’s realistic.

02

Design & layout

I help you nail the layout, materials and electrical setup before a single screw goes in — measure twice, cut once.

03

Build together

From a full hands-on build to weekend guidance while you do it yourself. As much or as little help as you need.

04

Hit the road

Final checks, a proper handover, and you’re off. I’m still on the end of the phone when questions pop up.

What I can help with

Whatever stage you’re at.

Just bought an empty panel van, or halfway through and hit a wall with the electrics? Either way, there’s a way I can help.

  • Full conversions, start to finish
  • Insulation, ply-lining & flooring
  • 12V electrics & solar setups
  • Water, gas & heating
  • Furniture & clever storage
  • One-off help when you’re stuck
