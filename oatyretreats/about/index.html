Nice to meet you

Hi, I’m Porridge.

Oaty’s Retreats is me, a Yorkshire lad, a van, a set of tools, and a slightly daft nickname that stuck. I’m converting my first camper from an empty shell into a proper home on wheels — and filming the whole thing so you can come along.

🥣

Why “Oaty’s Retreats”?

Mates have called me Porridge for years, so “Oaty” was always going to be in there somewhere. And a proper camper trip should be exactly that — a little retreat. Look closely and there’s a treat hiding right in the middle of it, which is the whole idea: a getaway that feels like a treat. Daft? A bit. But it makes me smile every time.

The plan

I’m doing this in the open, one step at a time:

  1. Build my own van first. Learn the craft properly by actually doing it — insulation, electrics, the lot — and document every step, mistakes included.
  2. Put her on the road. Once she’s finished, the first Oaty’s Retreats camper goes out for hire — with the Dales, the moors and the whole Yorkshire coast right on the doorstep to explore.
  3. Help you build yours. With a build (and a lot of lessons) under my belt, I’ll start helping other people convert their own vans — from full builds to a weekend of guidance.
I’d rather do one thing properly and show my working than pretend I’ve got it all figured out. Come learn with me.
